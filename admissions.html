Admissions

Joining the Wonder Whiz family

Four simple steps. No pressure, no sales pitch — just a proper chance to see whether we're the right fit for your family.

Admission Process

Four simple steps

Step 01
1

Schedule a tour or consultation

Visit us on a Tuesday or Thursday morning. 45 minutes to see the rooms, meet the team, and ask us anything.

Step 02
2

Submit registration form & documents

A short application plus the documents listed below. We'll respond within one working week.

Step 03
3

Pay registration & term fees

A one-time registration fee secures your child's place, followed by the term fee. Payment plans available.

Step 04
4

Orientation & settling-in phase

A gentle two-week settling plan led by your child's key person. Short visits, then longer stays — at their pace.

Documents Required

Please bring these with you when you visit, or email copies to hello@wonderwhiz.ae.

  • Passport copies (child + parents)
  • Emirates ID (child + parents)
  • Vaccination records
  • Passport size photos
