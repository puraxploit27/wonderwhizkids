Our Curriculum

Grounded in EYFS. Built for wonder.

The English Early Years Foundation Stage framework — delivered through play, curiosity, and real materials.

Early Years Foundation Stage (EYFS)

The EYFS framework sets the standards for the learning, development and care of children from birth to 5 years old in the UK — and we bring this excellence to Dubai.

7 Areas of Learning

What grows here

01

Personal, Social & Emotional Development

Helping children develop a positive sense of themselves and others.

02

Communication and Language

Fostering listening, understanding, and speaking skills in a language-rich environment.

03

Physical Development

Developing coordination, control, movement, and healthy habits.

04

Literacy and Mathematics

Exploring reading, writing, number sense, and patterns through play.

05

Understanding the World

Encouraging exploration, observation, and discussion about the world.

06

Expressive Arts and Design

Creating through music, movement, art, role play, and storytelling.

07

Holistic Growth

All seven areas woven together — because children don't learn in neat boxes.

Teaching Approach

How we teach

Not a checklist. A living approach that shapes every room, every routine, and every conversation.

1

Play-based & Child-initiated

Projects begin with a child's question, not a teacher's plan. We follow the thread wherever it leads.

2

Individualized Learning Plans

Every child is observed, documented, and gently challenged at exactly their own pace.

3

Observational Assessments

We watch, listen, photograph, and reflect. Progress is documented as a story, not a score.

4

School Readiness & Holistic Growth

Confident, compassionate, and curious — ready for FS2 and beyond, not just for a test.
